# EXECUTION PLAN — Stronger revision of BIOADV-2026-296

**"Near-duplicate leakage can reorder model rankings on a genomic benchmark suite: an audit of Genomic Benchmarks."**

Scope of this document: a single, phased execution plan that carries the paper from a rigorous but scoped **n=1 existence proof** (airtight ranking inversion on `human_enhancers_ensembl`, cautionary-partial on `human_nontata_promoters`, 13 Tier-1/2 experiments complete, foundation models honestly scoped out) to a **systematic, mechanistic, predictive contribution** that pre-empts the seven reviewer wires the last round rejected on. Every recommended dataset/model/method/tool below is grounded in a real, recent (2023–2026) paper or repository, verified against source (see §5 Citations).

---

## 0. Orienting judgment and recommended sequencing

The current paper is *correct and careful* but was rejected as *single-suite and model-thin*: reviewers read the ranking inversion as an anecdote (`n=1`) rather than a phenomenon, and demanded the modern models (CNN + DNABERT-2 + HyenaDNA) the audit did not run. The move that most raises it from **solid → strong** is therefore not another robustness check on the same suite — it is to (i) show the effect is **systematic** by replicating the identical audit on independently-constructed suites, and (ii) convert the descriptive finding into two **predictive laws** — *when* de-leaking inverts a ranking (Deepener #2) and *which* datasets leak by construction (Deepener #3) — so a reviewer sees a mechanism with forecasting power, not a curiosity. The decisive strategic asset is that the **confound-free backbone** (classical models + a from-scratch CNN + alignment-based homology tooling) already exists in this repo and can be ported to new suites with essentially no new modeling code, while the modern-model roster enters *only* where it is methodologically defensible (multi-species pretraining-disjoint subsets, leaderboard lower-bounds, and a pretraining-overlap-disentangled protocol) so we never re-trip the very confound the paper correctly named. **Recommended sequencing:** ship the two near-free predictive laws (#2, #3) and the certification tool skeleton (#6) plus the CPU-only cross-suite *leak census* (#1 phase A) **first** — they are all no-new-compute reanalysis/reuse and they pre-register the predictions that make the later cross-suite and leaderboard runs read as *prediction tests* rather than dataset fishing; then run the GPU-gated leaderboard-impact decomposition (#4) and protein/long-range reach tests (#6) and the foundation-model protocol (#5); finally take the big cross-suite swing (#1 full roster) and the optional genome-disjoint re-pretraining (#5 Tier-2). The single highest-leverage deepener is **#1 (cross-suite breadth)** because it answers the exact rejection basis and forces in the demanded model roster; #2 and #3 are the highest *impact-per-effort* wins and should be executed before #1 so that #1 becomes a registered multi-suite prediction test.

### Deepener #1 — Cross-suite breadth (the systematic claim)

**Goal.** Convert the `n=1` existence proof into a systematic finding by porting the paper's *exact* pipeline (homology census → graded-bin memorization → orthogonal whole-cluster re-split → ranking-inversion + P(rank-1) bootstrap) onto ≥3 independently-constructed suites. Simultaneously forces in the demanded model roster and supplies the natural experiment that most cleanly isolates curation from biology.

**Grounded SOTA approach.** A pre-registered, *tiered-by-predicted-leakage* audit:
- **T1 (predicted leaky):** GUE short human tasks — core-promoter (70 bp), TF-binding (100 bp), promoter (300 bp) — held-HP leaderboard in exactly the short fixed-length human-regulatory regime the paper flags (Zhou et al., DNABERT-2/GUE, ICLR 2024). Plus the **NT downstream tasks ORIGINAL vs REVISED natural experiment**: the revised card *literally* states it "replaced randomly generated negative samples by existing chunks of genomes" and gave "all datasets proper chromosome held-out test sets" (verified on HF), with `promoter_no_tata` directly comparable to the paper's `human_nontata_promoters`.
- **T2 (different mechanism, long-range):** Genomics Long-Range Benchmark (GLRB) variant-effect (ref/alt SNP pairs differ by one nucleotide; SNP-centered windows overlap) and `regulatory_element_enhancer`/`_promoter` (200 bp windows labeled by ≥50% overlap with annotated cis-REs — verified on HF) — a **test-to-test** leakage mechanism, unlike the paper's test-to-train.
- **T3 (clean controls):** BEND (80%-identity/chromosome-aware by construction) and GUE multi-species (yeast EMP, virus CVC) as within-suite negative controls; DeepSTARR (non-human, regression, genome-tiled) as a Spearman/Pearson reach linking to the tiling cause of #3.

**Homology definition (pre-empts circularity).** Primary = **hashFrag** (`filter_existing_splits` on the as-shipped split to measure the leak fraction the benchmark *ships with*; `stratify_test_split` to build similarity bins; `create_orthogonal_splits` union-find for the corrected split) — BLAST local-alignment identity, validated to 200 bp / 2 kb / 196 kb regimes (Rafi et al. 2025, verified v2). Cross-checked with **MMseqs2 easy-cluster** (`min-seq-id 0.7 -c 0.8`) and **CD-HIT**; the repo's 8-mer Jaccard + length-robust containment reported only as the fast lower bound.

**Model roster (pre-empts a, f).** Confound-free core on *every* dataset: LR, LinearSVC, default RF, HistGradientBoosting + from-scratch 1D residual CNN (reuse `audit/experiments/exp_deep.py`). De-confoundable subset adds fine-tuned **DNABERT-2 (117M)**, **HyenaDNA (1.6M)**, **NT-v2 (500M)**, **Caduceus-Ph (1.9M)**. The key trick: because HyenaDNA/Caduceus pretrain only on hg38, running them on **GUE yeast/virus/mouse** admits a real FM into the ranking audit with *provably zero pretraining overlap* — directly feeding #5.

**Concrete steps.**
1. Freeze provenance: pull GUE, NT-original + NT-revised, GLRB, BEND, DeepSTARR; build a per-dataset characteristics table (n, length distribution, class balance/prevalence, negative-sampling method [random/genomic-chunk/GAN-synthetic], split type [random/chromosome/identity-partitioned]).
2. Pre-register (timestamped, mirroring `results/deep_preregistration.md`): predicted-leaky list {GUE CPD/TF 70–100 bp, NT-original enhancers+promoter_no_tata, GLRB variant-effect, DeepSTARR} vs predicted-clean list {BEND, NT-revised, GUE yeast/virus}; primary metric per suite; refutation condition (strong "systematic" reading refuted if <2 datasets across <2 suites invert, or if any predicted-clean dataset inverts).
3. Homology census on the as-shipped split (hashFrag BLAST leak fraction + MMseqs2/CD-HIT cross-check + exact-duplicate census) → multi-suite report card.
4. Graded-bin memorization: hashFrag `stratify_test_split`, train core models on shipped split, plot acc/MCC vs similarity bin, report per-model graded gap acc[high]−acc[novel] + nearest-neighbor label concordance.
5. Orthogonal re-split (5 seeds, preserving ratio/balance/prevalence) → refit roster → record each model's score + rank pre/post under each suite's own primary metric AND the metric-robust set.
6. Foundation-model de-confounded ranking on the multi-species / NT-original-vs-revised subset (each suite's published HP recipe + small LR sweep).
7. Uncertainty + multiplicity (see below).
8. Winner test + synthesis: P(rank-1) bootstrap pre/post per dataset; near-replication panel comparing the paper's own `human_nontata_promoters`/`human_enhancers_ensembl` against NT `promoter_no_tata` / GLRB+NT enhancers (same biology, different curation) to separate task-effect from curation-effect.

**Metrics + correct uncertainty.** Each suite's own primary metric (MCC for GUE non-Covid + NT histone/enhancer; F1 for promoter/splice/Covid; Spearman/Pearson for DeepSTARR) reported alongside the full metric-robust set (accuracy, AUROC, F1, MCC). **Cluster/block bootstrap resampling whole within-test homology-connected components as blocks**, with **ICC + design effect reported per dataset** — and the explicit, load-bearing contrast that the design effect is *small* where near-duplicates are test-to-train (paper's regime, deff 1.06–1.17) but *large* where they are test-to-test (GLRB ref/alt pairs, DeepSTARR tiling), so the cluster bootstrap genuinely matters there. Combined-source CI folding 5-seed re-split variance; Liang–Zeger cluster-robust SE cross-check; P(rank-1) sample- and cluster-bootstrap; **Benjamini–Hochberg at q=0.05** across the enlarged multi-suite delta grid. Prevalence-aware metrics (AUPRC-vs-baseline, MCC, minority recall) on imbalanced sets.

**Pass criteria.** *Systematic* reading supported if a metric-robust inversion (best model changes; holds across ≥ accuracy/MCC + one threshold-free metric; P(rank-1) moves decisively; delta survives cluster bootstrap + BH) appears on **n≥2 datasets from ≥2 independent suites**, WHILE BEND and every predicted-clean dataset show no inversion / null deltas. Secondary: NT-original-vs-revised shows the leak fraction + any inversion present in original and absent/attenuated in revised. Honest-null is publishable: if no second suite inverts, the paper reports the multi-suite report card and retains the scoped `n=1` claim, now externally stress-tested — the pre-registered refutation makes either outcome a clean contribution.

**Datasets/models/tools.** GUE (Zhou 2024); NT original+revised (Dalla-Torre 2024/2025, HF InstaDeepAI); GLRB (InstaDeep NeurIPS 2024); BEND (Marin 2024); DeepSTARR (de Almeida 2022, Zenodo 5502060); GenBench/GUANinE as extra ranking references. Homology: hashFrag, MMseqs2, CD-HIT. Models: classical + CNN + DNABERT-2 + HyenaDNA + NT-v2 + Caduceus.

**Compute/effort.** Confound-free backbone across ~30–40 datasets: CPU + single GPU/MPS, ~2–4 weeks, reusing the repo's audit code essentially unchanged (work is data ingestion + re-runs). hashFrag BLAST is CPU-bound but designed for it (hours–days/suite). FM roster: HyenaDNA/Caduceus tiny (hours/task); DNABERT-2/NT-v2 the cost drivers → scope to the ~10–12-cell de-confoundable subset, ~3–6 weeks. Classical+CNN cross-suite report card alone (2–4 weeks) already delivers the `n>1` systematic claim if the FM subset slips.

**Risks + mitigations.** *"Re-ran your k-mer trick on more data"* → hashFrag BLAST is primary, MMseqs2/CD-HIT cross-check, k-mer only a bound. *Cherry-picking* → timestamped pre-registration + BEND clean control + full report card. *FM pretraining re-contaminates* → classical+CNN carry the systematic claim; FMs only on genome-disjoint multi-species subsets (coordinate with #5). *Over-correction / chaining* (the nontata failure mode) → sweep identity threshold, report cohesion diagnostics. *Under-documented GUE/NT provenance* → treat the as-shipped split as ground truth for the census regardless of how it was made.

**Key citations.** Zhou et al. 2024 (DNABERT-2/GUE); Dalla-Torre et al. 2025 (NT, Nat Methods) + NT-revised HF card; InstaDeep 2024 (GLRB); Marin et al. 2024 (BEND); de Almeida et al. 2022 (DeepSTARR); Rafi et al. 2025 (hashFrag); Steinegger & Söding 2017 (MMseqs2); Li & Godzik 2006 (CD-HIT); Tahir et al. 2024 (LOCO-EPI ranking-change precedent).

### Deepener #2 — Theoretical inversion condition (the predictive law) — **Tier-1, no new compute**

**Goal.** Turn the empirical inversion into a closed-form, out-of-sample-validated condition that predicts *both* the `ensembl` inversion *and* the `nontata` non-inversion from the *same* inequality, and forecasts which model-pair × dataset × regularization × contamination settings invert *before* the corrected split is computed.

**Grounded SOTA derivation.** For a fixed trained model M on the leaky test set, stratify test into a leaked stratum (near-duplicate, fraction φ) and a novel stratum (1−φ): a_M = φ·h_M + (1−φ)·n_M. The near-duplicate-aware splitter removes exactly the cross-split near-duplicates, so a_M^corr ≈ n_M. Hence the **per-model drop is the paper's existing decomposition, now derived:** Δ_M = a_M − a_M^corr = φ·(h_M − n_M) = φ·g_M, with g_M the graded memorization gap. For models A (leaky leader) and B (challenger): original margin μ = a_A − a_B; corrected margin μ′ = n_A − n_B = −δ (δ = B's true novel-sequence advantage). **Inversion iff 0 < δ < φ·Δg** (Δg = g_A − g_B), equivalently the fully-observable assumption-free form **0 < μ < Δ_A − Δ_B**. Critical leak fraction **φ\* = δ/Δg** is a *ranking breakdown point* (order-inversal breakdown point lineage, Werner et al. 2021); the ranking inverts iff φ > φ\*. Continuous generalization: Δ_M = ∫(ρ(s)−ρ′(s))·acc_M(s) ds over similarity s, with hashFrag's stratified acc(s) curves instantiating acc_M(s).

**Anchor validation (numbers from frozen results).** `ensembl` A=RF, B=LinearSVC: n_RF=0.770, n_SVC=0.782 ⇒ δ=+0.012 (small, >0); g_RF=0.230, g_SVC≈0.035 ⇒ Δg=0.195; **φ\*=0.062**. Measured φ = 0.384 (Jaccard) / 0.845 (containment) / ~0.71 (effective) — all ≫ φ\* ⇒ **inversion predicted under every homology metric**; observable form 0 < μ(0.062) < Δ_RF−Δ_SVC(0.162). Matches the observed RF→LinearSVC flip. `nontata`: under chromosome-holdout + threshold-free AUROC, RF stays rank 1, so on genuinely novel sequences δ ≤ 0 (accuracy-only value +0.009 has a CI straddling 0); condition (1) fails ⇒ **no clean inversion predicted**. The *same* inequality explains both cases, with the sign/robustness of δ as the discriminator — the SOTA move: a predictive law, not another robustness check.

**Concrete steps** (all CPU reanalysis of existing artifacts).
1. Write the formal note (Methods + short Theory subsection): stratified identity, Δ_M=φ·g_M, inequality 0<δ<φ·Δg, observable form 0<μ<Δ_A−Δ_B, φ\*=δ/Δg, continuous acc(s) form; state the one assumption (a_corr≈n_novel) explicitly.
2. Anchor validation: plug measured (φ, g, n) from `results/graded_performance.csv`, `exp_rankings.csv`, `reconciled_numbers.md` for ensembl (RF vs LinearSVC) and nontata (RF vs LR).
3. Pre-register (timestamped `results/inversion_preregistration.md`): freeze the inequality + predicted φ\* and corrected-margin sign for every pair × dataset from ORIGINAL-split-only measurements.
4. Predict-then-reveal across all 6 classical pairs × 7 binary + 3-class: compute (φ, g_A, g_B, δ) from original-split correctness vectors, predict inversion sign, reveal corrected ranking, score as a confusion matrix.
5. **Out-of-sample sweep #1 (g-axis, reuse `results/exp_regpath.csv`):** as RF is regularized (min_samples_leaf ∈{1,5,20,50,100,200,500} × max_depth ∈{None,16,8,4}), predict the standard-split rank at each of ~28 cells from (φ, g_RF(cell), δ) with **no free parameters**; check against the observed `rf_rank_orig` crossover (1 for msl≤5, 4 for msl≥20). *This is the hyperparameter-wire answer.*
6. **Out-of-sample sweep #2 (φ-axis, reuse `results/exp_inject3class.csv`):** from injected-leakage multiclass drops (f∈{0,0.1,0.2,0.4}), estimate φ(f), predict critical injection f\* solving φ(f)·Δg=δ, check predicted RF-vs-LR order at each f.
7. Metric-invariance: recompute φ, g under MMseqs2/BLAST alignment (`results/exp_alignment.csv`), re-derive φ\*, show ensembl stays inverted / nontata stays non-inverted.
8. Model-agnostic instantiation: validate Δ=φ·g on the CNN dose-response cells (`results/exp_deep_cnn.csv`; unreg-CNN gap 0.222 ≈ unreg-RF 0.230); then a clearly-labelled FORECAST for FMs via RF-on-embeddings (φ, g) with effective φ→1 under ~100% pretraining overlap.
9. Uncertainty (below).
10. Reach: instantiate (φ, g, δ) on one GraphPart/CD-HIT-partitioned protein task and one long-range task via hashFrag's Enformer-regime (196 kb) stratified curves.
11. Figure: phase diagram in (φ, δ/Δg) space with the φ\*=δ/Δg boundary, every pair × dataset × reg-path × injection point colored by predicted-vs-observed inversion.

**Metrics + correct uncertainty.** Predictive-accuracy: confusion matrix of predicted-vs-observed inversion; predicted-vs-observed critical min_samples_leaf; predicted-vs-observed f\*. Ranking readout: **pairwise sign agreement / Spearman, NOT Kendall τ** — the condition is pairwise by construction, which *neutralizes the standing "only 4 models, τ≈0" weakness*. Uncertainty: **wild cluster bootstrap-t + pairs cluster bootstrap (Cameron–Gelbach–Miller 2008)**, resampling whole within-test near-duplicate components as blocks, 10k reps, percentile CIs on m_inv=φ·Δg−δ and on δ; cross-check the analytic Liang–Zeger CRVE and the paper's existing combined-source interval (reuse `cluster_bootstrap_full.py`). Decision: CLEAN iff lower-CI(m_inv)>0 AND lower-CI(δ)>0; FRAGILE iff δ CI straddles 0 — recovering ensembl=clean, nontata=fragile *from the theory*. BH across pairs.

**Pass criteria.** Same inequality predicts ensembl inversion (φ≫φ\*=0.062 under all three metrics) AND nontata non-inversion (δ CI straddles 0), with no dataset-specific fitting; predicted critical regularization matches the observed `rf_rank_orig` crossover (predicted msl\* ∈ [5,20]) with predict-then-reveal ≥90% across ~28 cells; predicted f\* matches the observed RF-vs-LR order flip; zero false "clean-inversion" calls on clean/borderline datasets; every CLEAN predicted inversion has lower-CI(m_inv)>0 and survives BH. Pre-registered fallback: if the observable form and the φ·g form disagree on any anchor (expected on nontata under chaining, cohesion 0.083), report the assumption violation and use the observable form as primary.

**Datasets/models/tools.** GB binary + 3-class (anchors ensembl, nontata); reach = one GraphPart/CD-HIT protein task + hashFrag Enformer-regime curves. Models: the paper's classical suite + CNN; FM forecast via RF-on-embeddings (Feng et al. 2025 protocol — *verified*: RF on zero-shot mean-token embeddings of DNABERT-2/NT-v2/HyenaDNA/Caduceus-Ph). Stats: wild/pairs cluster bootstrap, BH, CRVE. **Reused artifacts (all present on disk):** `results/{exp_regpath.csv, exp_inject3class.csv, exp_alignment.csv, exp_deep_cnn.csv, exp_rankings.csv, exp_rank_prob.csv, graded_performance.csv, novelonly_ranking.csv, cluster_bootstrap_full.csv, reconciled_numbers.md}`.

**Compute/effort.** CPU-only reanalysis; seconds–minutes/step; the 10k wild-cluster-bootstrap a few CPU-minutes. **~1–2 analyst-days for the defensible contribution (steps 1–9, 11), no GPU.** Optional reach/forecast (protein probe + FM embeddings) ~1–3 GPU-days, additive not prerequisite.

**Risks + mitigations.** Assumption a_corr≈n_novel imperfect → observable form is primary; report residual |a_corr−n_novel|. Circularity (g, φ measured on the same split) → predict-then-reveal + pre-registration + two genuine out-of-sample sweeps with no free parameters. Two-bin coarseness → continuous acc(s) form on hashFrag curves. Few models → pairwise CIs + clean/fragile classification. *"Obvious algebra"* → position against the order-inversal breakdown point (Werner 2021) and accuracy-on-the-line (Miller 2021); novelty = the closed-form φ\* ranking breakdown point for near-duplicate benchmark leakage + its validated predictive use, which no prior genomics-leakage work provides. FM forecast → labelled a forecast, backed by the runnable RF-on-embeddings test, never reported as a clean split-effect number.

**Key citations.** Kapoor & Narayanan 2023 (leakage can change which method is best); Lewis et al. 2021 (empirical drop = overlap×acc-on-seen decomposition, memorizer outranks generalizer); Rafi et al. 2025 (acc-vs-similarity genomic anchor); Feldman 2020 / Feldman & Zhang 2020 (memorization propensity g is a real model property); Miller et al. 2021 (accuracy-on-the-line / off-the-line regime); Werner et al. 2021 (order-inversal breakdown point); Cameron, Gelbach & Miller 2008 (wild cluster bootstrap-t); Lee et al. 2022 (leakage biases HP selection toward memorizers); Feng et al. 2025 (RF-on-FM-embeddings).

### Deepener #3 — Construction rule (which benchmarks leak, predictively) — **Tier-1, no new compute**

**Goal.** A predictive, falsifiable *cause* of leakage from provenance + recovered-coordinate geometry, validated by construct-and-break manipulation. **Core thesis (the non-obvious, defensible point):** leakage is caused not by "multi-cell-type data" but by whether the positive class is an **un-merged union of experimentally-called regions that tiles recurring loci** (leaky) vs a **merged non-overlapping segmentation / deduplicated set / random-genome draw** (clean). The decisive contrast is *internal to Ensembl*: `human_enhancers_ensembl` (LEAKY, 38% byte-identical) is the raw FANTOM5 enhancer union across 808 CAGE libraries, whereas `human_ocr_ensembl` and `human_ensembl_regulatory` (CLEAN) come from the Ensembl Regulatory Build — an explicitly *merged*, cell-type-collapsed "MultiCell" consensus. `human_nontata_promoters` (LEAKY, near-dup not exact) is EPDnew fixed 251 bp TSS windows with the community-standard CD-HIT-EST 0.8 redundancy filter *omitted*.

**Grounded SOTA approach (three layers, all CPU).**
- **Layer 1 — provenance taxonomy:** classify each of the 7 binary + 1 three-class datasets by how the positive class was assembled, read from the GB `docs/` construction notebooks + primary sources: {un-merged multi-sample atlas union (FANTOM5 enhancers), fixed-window-over-recurring-loci without redundancy filtering (EPDnew nontata), merged segmentation consensus (Ensembl Regulatory Build → ocr, 3-class), deduplicated curated set (Cohn, Stark), random-genome draw (demo_coding, demo_human_or_worm)}. Record whether a merge/redundancy/CD-HIT step was applied.
- **Layer 2 — coordinate-space construction signatures** (computed with **pyranges** directly from the shipped BED-like CSVs — causally upstream of and independent from the sequence-space clustering): (a) self-overlap redundancy R_self = 1 − merged/raw; (b) duplicate/near-dup coordinate fraction via `cluster_overlaps` at reciprocal 0.5/0.9/1.0; (c) cross-split TEST→TRAIN coordinate-overlap leak fraction at ≥1 bp/50%/90%; (d) per-CAGE-library multiplicity of each enhancer duplicate (same enhancer across many cell types vs distinct closely-spaced enhancers).
- **Layer 3 — construct-and-break manipulations** (the strongest evidence, mirroring the paper's injected-leakage logic): **(A) break-a-clean** — rebuild `human_ocr_ensembl`/3-class from per-cell-type Ensembl segmentation OCR peaks (raw union) instead of the merged consensus, predict it flips to LEAKY with RF inflation; **(B) fix-a-leaky** — apply pyranges `merge_overlaps` OR CD-HIT-EST 0.8 (the omitted EPDnew filter) to positives before splitting, predict leakage → ~0 and the RF demotion vanishes — two mechanistically independent fixes converging. Cross-validate the coordinate cause against the sequence effect with four independent detectors (pyranges coordinate overlap, CD-HIT-EST 0.8, MMseqs2 easy-cluster, hashFrag BLASTn) to defuse k-mer circularity.

**Concrete steps.** (1) Pull shipped coordinate CSVs for all datasets. (2) Assign provenance category from `docs/` + primary sources. (3) Compute Layer-2 metrics per dataset, positives and negatives separately, to localize redundancy to the positive class. (4) For ensembl, recover per-window FANTOM5 sample provenance; for nontata, group windows by nearest gene/paralog family and regress near-dup fraction on within-family window count. (5) Four-detector cross-check. (6) Manipulation A. (7) Manipulation B. (8) Fit a transparent decision rule over {provenance, R_self, cross-split overlap} → {LEAKY, borderline, CLEAN}, evaluated by leave-one-dataset-out (NOT a learned classifier — n=8 is too small for a credible CI). (9) Write the certification checklist and an extended report-card column (provenance / R_self / predicted verdict).

**Metrics + correct uncertainty.** Coordinate signatures (R_self, duplicate fraction, cross-split overlap) as the independent variable; effect metrics unchanged (near-dup leak, best-model drop, RF rank change, graded gap). Agreement: Spearman + threshold-classification concordance between the coordinate cross-split-overlap metric and each of the four sequence detectors. **The dataset is the unit and n=8 is too small for a fitted-classifier CI** — so report leave-one-dataset-out separation + per-dataset point values and carry the causal claim with the two manipulations, not a regression CI. Within-dataset: **cluster (block) bootstrap resampling whole coordinate-overlap components** for a CI on the cross-split-overlap fraction and on the manipulation-induced RF-drop change; family-clustered bootstrap for the nontata paralog dose-response slope.

**Pass criteria.** Provenance cleanly separates: the two un-merged-union/unfiltered datasets are exactly the LEAKY ones; merged-segmentation, curated, and random-genome are exactly CLEAN (with demo_coding's coding-paralog homology explaining its containment-only borderline status). Coordinate metric concordant with all four sequence detectors on all 8 datasets. Leave-one-dataset-out preserves the held-out verdict. Manipulation A re-introduces RF inflation (cluster-bootstrap CI on induced drop-change excludes 0); Manipulation B drives leak → ~0 and removes the demotion under both fixes. FAILS (reported) if a merged/curated dataset shows high R_self, if the fixes don't remove the demotion, or if coordinate and sequence detectors disagree.

**Datasets/models/tools.** GB 7 binary + 3-class (BED-like coordinate CSVs). Provenance sources: FANTOM5 (Andersson 2014), Ensembl Regulatory Build (Zerbino 2015), EPD/EPDnew (Dreos 2013/2015; Umarov & Solovyev 2017), Cohn 2018, Stark. Coordinate geometry: pyranges (Stovner & Sætrom 2020) / bedtools (Quinlan & Hall 2010). Sequence cross-check + fix: CD-HIT-EST (Li & Godzik 2006), MMseqs2 (Steinegger 2017), hashFrag (Rafi 2025), GraphPart (Teufel 2023). Models for the re-audit: the repo's 4 classical + from-scratch CNN. Framing: Whalen et al. 2022 (dependency-structure pitfall, overlapping-window example), Kapoor & Narayanan 2023.

**Compute/effort.** CPU-only, no GPU. pyranges on ≤175k intervals = seconds–minutes/dataset; CD-HIT-EST/MMseqs2 on short regulatory windows = minutes–low hours/dataset; hashFrag BLASTn a few CPU-hours/dataset. Manipulation A needs Ensembl per-cell-type segmentation tracks (a few GB); B is pure reprocessing. ### Deepener #4 — Published-leaderboard impact (the real-world casualty)

**Goal.** Show a *real, published* ranking on the two leaky GB datasets is leakage-inflated: the leaderboard-topping model's margin sits on near-duplicate contamination, and a homology-aware re-evaluation reorders it or collapses its margins into noise. Converts "you scoped out foundation models" into the headline that the published FM leaderboard is the casualty.

**Grounded SOTA approach.** Three-condition re-evaluation of three published rankings on identical test IDs:
- **L1** — HyenaDNA (NeurIPS 2023) Table 4.1 (CNN/DNABERT/GPT/HyenaDNA), the repo's officially-cited "current SOTA"; its headline "+20 accuracy points on enhancer identification" (68.9→89.2) is *exactly* `human_enhancers_ensembl` — the dataset the paper shows is **38% byte-identical**.
- **L2** — GenBench (NeurIPS 2024) 7-model table (top model differs per dataset, top-4 spread ~0.02) — *verified suite spans Genomic Benchmarks + GUE + DeepSTARR with CNN/HyenaDNA/DNABERT-2/NT/Caduceus/GENA-LM*.
- **L3** — Feng et al. (Nat Commun 16:10780, 2025) 5-model table (DNABERT-2/NT-v2/HyenaDNA/Caduceus-Ph/GROVER, RF on zero-shot mean-token embeddings, already DeLong-adjudicated at p<0.01) — *verified*; AUC spreads this small on a 38%-duplicated test set are the textbook de-leaking case.

Three test conditions per model (its own published recipe): **(A) standard split** (reproduce published number, anchored to public reference values); **(B) novel-only** (drop test sequences with ≥0.9 near-dup / MMseqs2+hashFrag homology to train — inference-only, no retraining); **(C) corrected split** (retrain on de-leaked training set). Two deliverables: **D1 leakage-attributable-margin decomposition** (HyenaDNA's +20-pt enhancer margin and the Feng 0.006–0.008 inter-model margins shrink/invert once the 38%-duplicated partition is removed) and **D2 corrected-split re-ranking** (≥1 reported top-2 or top-vs-median ordering flips or becomes statistically indistinguishable). Present the **from-scratch subset (GB-CNN, GPT-scratch, our 1D CNN) as the confound-free re-ranking** and the **genome-pretrained subset as explicit lower bounds** — consistent with the paper's §6 scoping.

**Concrete steps.** (0) Lock L1/L2/L3 numbers; reproduce GB-CNN baseline + one anchor FM number before touching splits. (1) Build homology-certified partitions on the published test splits (reuse the 38% census + 8-mer clusters; add MMseqs2 + hashFrag BLAST for leaked ≥0.9 / intermediate / novel <0.5; verify leaked-partition label concordance ~99.99%/99.95%). (2) Fine-tune CNN-scratch + DNABERT-2 + HyenaDNA + NT-v2 (+Caduceus-Ph if compute allows) with each model's official recipe, 3–5 seeds. (3) D1 (inference-only): score every model on full/novel-only/per-bin; report acc/AUROC/F1/MCC + graded acc-vs-similarity curve per model; compute leakage-attributable fraction of each score and each top-vs-next margin. (4) D2: re-fine-tune on the corrected split, produce the corrected leaderboard, identify flips. (5) Ranking stats (below). (6) Confound partitioning + honest framing. (7) One-suite GUE reach (bridge to #1). (8) Release de-leaked test-ID lists + per-model per-example prediction vectors so any leaderboard can be re-scored without retraining.

**Metrics + correct uncertainty.** Per model × split: accuracy, AUROC, F1, MCC (MCC/AUPRC foreground the effect under the curated-balance caveat). Ranking inference: **(i) paired DeLong test for correlated AUCs (DeLong 1988)**; **(ii) cluster/block bootstrap resampling whole near-duplicate homology clusters** (Pang et al. 2023 — the ordinary bootstrap under-covers correlated-AUC CIs at 63–82% vs nominal 95%; cluster bootstrap restores coverage) on the paired accuracy AND AUC difference; **(iii) P(rank-1) bootstrap** (reuse the paper's G6 estimator) on standard/novel-only/corrected; (iv) Kendall τ + rank-displacement, reported *with* the standing caveat that τ is too coarse for ≤7 models — so per-pair flips + P(rank-1) lead. **BH at q=0.05** across the leaderboard's model pairs. Effect-decomposition headline: leakage-attributable margin = (top−next on full test) − (top−next on novel-only), as a fraction of the published margin. Power reported honestly: at ΔAUC ~0.006–0.008 DeLong is underpowered unless predictions are highly correlated, so "the published margin is within noise" is stated as a positive finding.

**Pass criteria.** PRIMARY (any one, per dataset): a reported top-2/top-vs-median ordering flips under novel-only OR corrected; OR the #1-vs-#2 margin becomes indistinguishable (DeLong p>0.05 AND cluster-bootstrap paired-difference CI includes 0); OR the HyenaDNA +20-pt enhancer lead collapses ≥50% when the leaked partition is removed. SECONDARY (confound-free, must-hold): among from-scratch models the corrected-split ranking differs from standard on `human_enhancers_ensembl` (mirrors the airtight RF→LinearSVC result). Honest-negative path: if all orderings survive, report that these FM rankings are leakage-*robust* and quantify how large a margin must be to be leakage-safe on a 38%-duplicated test set — still decision-useful.

**Datasets/models/tools.** `human_enhancers_ensembl` + `human_nontata_promoters` (primary); GUE promoter/core-promoter (reach). Rankings: HyenaDNA NeurIPS-2023 Table 4.1, GenBench NeurIPS-2024, Feng et al. Nat Commun 2025. Models: GB-CNN + our 1D CNN + GPT-scratch (confound-free block); DNABERT-2, NT-v2-50M/100M, HyenaDNA-tiny/-1k, Caduceus-Ph, GENA-LM (lower-bound block). De-leaking: MMseqs2, hashFrag BLAST, + the repo's exact-dup census / 8-mer clusters / splitter. Stats: DeLong, cluster/hierarchical bootstrap (Pang 2023), P(rank-1), BH.

**Compute/effort.** GPU-gated but bounded. D1 is inference-only (hours on one consumer GPU once checkpoints exist, no retraining). D2 fine-tuning on ≤500 bp binary tasks: HyenaDNA/DNABERT-2 ≪1 GPU-hr each, NT-v2-50M/100M a few GPU-hrs; ~100–200 fine-tune runs total → a few days on a single A100/4090. NT-v2-500M/Caduceus-Ph optional. **Lowest-cost high-leverage path: ship D1 alone** — it already delivers "HyenaDNA's +20-pt SOTA sits on 38% duplication." ~2–3 focused weeks including writeup.

**Risks + mitigations.** Can't reproduce published fine-tunes exactly → use official recipes/checkpoints, anchor to public reference numbers, always report reproduced-standard next to published; the claim is the *delta between splits on our own reproduced models*. Pretraining confound → foreground from-scratch re-ranking; label pretrained drops as lower bounds; defer clean pretrained claim to #5. Tiny margins underpowered → that *is* the finding; never claim a flip without both p>0.05 and a CI crossing 0. nontata chaining → report ensembl as primary, nontata as cautionary secondary. Scooping by hashFrag/GenBench → neither does a de-leaked *re-ranking* of a specific published GB classification leaderboard with the leaked-margin decomposition; cite both, use hashFrag as the engine.

**Key citations.** Nguyen et al. 2023 (HyenaDNA, repo SOTA); Liu et al. 2024 (GenBench); Feng et al. 2025 (Nat Commun leaderboard); Rafi et al. 2025 (hashFrag); Pang et al. 2023 (cluster bootstrap for correlated AUC); DeLong et al. 1988; Dehghani et al. 2021 (Benchmark Lottery); Zhou et al. 2024 (GUE).

### Deepener #5 — PODA: Pretraining-Overlap-Disentangled Assessment (turns the limitation into a protocol)

**Goal.** A reusable FM evaluation protocol that separates the near-duplicate **split-leakage** channel (Δ_split, the paper's channel) from a distinct **pretraining-overlap** channel (Δ_pretrain) that *no re-split can close*, executed on DNABERT-2, HyenaDNA, NT-v2, GENA-LM, Caduceus. Converts the Discussion's "genuinely open" statement into evidence, and shows *why the reviewer's naive fine-tune-on-standard-split request is itself confounded* — supplying the corrected protocol.

**Grounded SOTA approach.** Decompose apparent accuracy into generalization + Δ_split (original − corrected acc) + **Δ_pretrain (acc on pretraining-SEEN test chromosomes − acc on pretraining-HELD-OUT test chromosomes, on the corrected split)**. Three tiers:
- **Tier 0 (inference only; also removes fine-tuning HPs):** (0a) frozen mean-token-embedding graded memorization gap acc[≥0.9]−acc[<0.5] with a light probe (Feng et al. 2025 verified mean-token pooling is SOTA for GB-style tasks); (0b) reference-free membership via **Min-K% Prob** (Shi et al. 2024) + LOSS/perplexity + likelihood-ratio MIA, scoring near-dup vs novel (and, for held-out-chromosome models, seen vs held-out chromosomes as gold labels); (0c) embedding near-duplicate collapse (cosine of near-dup vs nearest-train-neighbor embeddings).
- **Tier 1 (LoRA fine-tuning of released checkpoints; headline):** Channel A — Δ_split for each FM vs the from-scratch CNN and default RF; Channel B — partition the corrected test by hg38 chromosome into pretraining-SEEN vs HELD-OUT per checkpoint (**HyenaDNA/Caduceus → Enformer/Basenji chr14/chrX held-out** — *verified: HyenaDNA pretrains on hg38 via Basenji `human-sequences.bed` which carries a split column*; GENA-LM → its documented held-out chromosomes; DNABERT-2 & NT → no held-out human = "fully-contaminated" reference), fine-tune on seen-chromosome training data, evaluate acc(seen)−acc(held-out)=Δ_pretrain.
- **Tier 2 (genome-disjoint re-pretraining; causal clincher, optional):** re-pretrain HyenaDNA-1K from scratch on hg38 with the leaky datasets' source chromosomes excluded; the released model scoring higher only on high-similarity bins causally attributes the gap to pretraining memorization.

Controls throughout: from-scratch CNN (Δ_pretrain≈0 by construction) and random-init FM (isolates pretraining's contribution — grounded in Vishniakov et al. 2024, embeddings retain >0.99 cosine under half-nucleotide changes; random-init GFMs often match pretrained).

**Concrete steps.** (1) Pre-register the decomposition, grid, LoRA LR sweep, and the SEEN/HELD-OUT chromosome map + refutation condition. (2) Build the pretraining-overlap partition: reuse the hg38 coordinates already recovered for the chromosome-holdout control; download Enformer/Basenji interval splits (Genentech/enformer-data) + GENA-LM held-out lists; bedtools-intersect each test interval to label SEEN/HELD-OUT per model. (3) Tier-0 frozen embeddings (+ random-init copies) → graded gaps. (4) Tier-0 Min-K%/LOSS/MIA membership AUROCs. (5) Tier-0 embedding collapse. (6) Tier-1 LoRA Channel A (Δ_split). (7) Tier-1 Channel B (Δ_pretrain). (8) Optional Tier-2 re-pretraining. (9) Stats. (10) Promote the Discussion paragraph to a Results subsection + a decomposition figure + release a dependency-light `podacheck` tool.

**Metrics + correct uncertainty.** Δ_split, Δ_pretrain, graded gap, membership AUROC (Min-K%, LOSS, MIA). accuracy/AUROC/F1/MCC (MCC/AUPRC for nontata mild imbalance). **Cluster (block) bootstrap resampling whole within-test near-duplicate components** for every gap; bootstrap AUROCs over the same clusters; ICC/design-effect as in the paper. **BH at q=0.05** across the model×dataset×metric grid. Effect claimed only when the cluster-bootstrap CI excludes 0 (gaps) or 0.5 (AUROC) and survives BH. From-scratch CNN + random-init FM are negative controls (Δ_pretrain CI must include 0); a clean dataset's Δ_split, Δ_pretrain must include 0.

**Pass criteria.** Strong pass: on `human_enhancers_ensembl`, ≥1 released FM has Δ_pretrain CI excluding 0 (higher on seen than held-out chromosomes) AND membership AUROC >0.5 separating near-dups from novel, while the from-scratch CNN's Δ_pretrain CI includes 0. Tier-2 clincher: the genome-disjoint re-pretrained HyenaDNA loses the high-similarity-bin advantage. Pre-registered refutation (reported either way): the strong reading is refuted if Δ_pretrain CI includes 0 on both leaky datasets for the majority of held-out-chromosome checkpoints — in which case the honest finding is "Δ_split reaches FMs but Δ_pretrain is not separately detectable at this scale," which still answers the model-scope wire and still corrects the naive-fine-tuning request. Either outcome is publishable; the protocol + tool is the contribution.

**Datasets/models/tools.** `human_enhancers_ensembl`, `human_nontata_promoters` + a clean control (`human_ocr_ensembl` / `demo_human_or_worm`). Models (HF): DNABERT-2-117M, HyenaDNA-{tiny-1k,small-32k}, NT-v2-500M-multi-species, GENA-LM-bert-base-t2t, Caduceus-Ph + random-init copies. Pretraining-region refs: Enformer/Basenji splits (Avsec et al. 2021; Genentech/enformer-data), GENA-LM held-out lists (Fishman et al. 2025). Methods: Min-K% Prob (Shi et al. 2024), LoRA (Hu et al. 2022), mean-token pooling (Feng et al. 2025), MMseqs2 near-dup partition (already in paper), bedtools, the repo's cluster-bootstrap + `homology_split.py`.

**Compute/effort.** Tier 0 (inference only): embeddings + per-token likelihoods for ~5 FMs × 3 datasets — a few GPU-hours/model on a single 24 GB GPU, no training, de-confounds HPs → the minimum viable version. Tier 1 (LoRA): 5 FMs × 3 datasets × 4 LRs × ~3 seeds ≈ tens–low-hundreds of GPU-hours; each run minutes–hours on one A100. Tier 2 (re-pretraining): the largest cost, ~1–2 days on one 8×A100 node (or a smaller variant) — optional, gold-standard. Tier 0+1 fit a single-GPU academic budget and deliver the headline.

**Risks + mitigations.** Small held-out-chromosome counts → pool multiple checkpoints; use the fully-contaminated DNABERT-2/NT reference as upper-bound contrast; fall back to Tier-0 membership AUROC (uses all sequences). Chromosome-specific difficulty confound → the from-scratch CNN on the same seen-chromosome data is the matched control (if its held-out acc also drops, attribute to shift not memorization) + the paper's GC-in-distribution control. LoRA under-fitting → LR sweep + a full-fine-tune check on the smallest model; frame as within-model seen-vs-held-out (self-normalized), not cross-model absolute wins. Min-K% is autoregressive → use pseudo-log-likelihood for masked models (DNABERT-2/NT/GENA-LM/Caduceus), native NLL for HyenaDNA; report both LOSS and Min-K%. Tier-2 scope creep → explicitly optional. Novelty → prior FM critiques (DART-Eval/ConvNova/Vishniakov) evaluate on the *standard leaky split* and never isolate the pretraining-overlap channel; hashFrag homology-splits from-scratch models; GLM-memorization work studies privacy canaries — PODA is the first to separate the two inflation channels for a downstream classification ranking.

**Key citations.** Nguyen et al. 2023 (HyenaDNA hg38 pretraining, chr14/chrX held out); Avsec et al. 2021 (Enformer/Basenji interval splits); Fishman et al. 2025 (GENA-LM held-out chromosomes); Zhou et al. 2024 (DNABERT-2 corpus), Dalla-Torre et al. 2025 (NT corpus); Shi et al. 2024 (Min-K% Prob); Hu et al. 2022 (LoRA); Feng et al. 2025 (mean-token pooling, RF-on-embeddings); Vishniakov et al. 2024 (embedding perturbation-insensitivity); Rafi et al. 2025 (hashFrag); Grešová et al. 2023 + Zerbino et al. 2015 (provenance of the leaky datasets → hg38 origin of the overlap).

### Deepener #6 — Build-and-Certify guideline + protein & long-range reach tests

## 4. How this pre-empts each reviewer wire (recap)

- **(a) Limited/outdated model scope.** #1 runs DNABERT-2 + HyenaDNA + NT-v2 + Caduceus + from-scratch CNN + classical on the suites those models were built for; #4 places exactly CNN + DNABERT-2 + HyenaDNA (+NT-v2/Caduceus/GENA-LM) in the leaderboard-reordering + lower-bound role; #5 evaluates all five FMs *correctly* and shows the naive fine-tune-on-standard-split request is itself confounded; #2 forecasts FM behavior under the quantified ~100% overlap and instantiates RF-on-embeddings. - **(b) Homology-detection validation.** hashFrag BLAST local-alignment is the PRIMARY detector across #1/#3/#4/#5/#6, cross-checked with MMseqs2 + CD-HIT + GraphPart (Needleman–Wunsch); 8-mer Jaccard/containment demoted to fast bounds; #3 adds a coordinate-space cause validated against four independent sequence detectors.
- **(c) Bootstrap methodology.** Cluster/block bootstrap over homology-connected components everywhere, with ICC + design effect per dataset; #1 makes the explicit contrast that the design effect is small for test-to-train leakage (the paper's regime) but large for test-to-test (GLRB/DeepSTARR), where the cluster bootstrap is load-bearing; #2 uses the wild cluster bootstrap-t (Cameron–Gelbach–Miller); #4 uses paired DeLong + cluster bootstrap for correlated AUC (Pang 2023); BH at q=0.05 across every enlarged grid.
- **(d) Dataset characteristics.** #1 and #3 tabulate per-dataset provenance / balance / prevalence / length / negative-sampling / split-type; #3 makes provenance the independent variable and reads it from the datasets' own construction notebooks + primary sources; prevalence-aware metrics throughout.
- **(e) Hyperparameters.** #2 turns the reg-path into a *quantitative prediction* that the theory forecasts where the inversion appears/disappears as RF is tuned ("we predict the effect of the full HP sweep and it matches"); #4/#5 fine-tune FMs with each model's *own official recipe* + a sweep; #5 Tier-0 removes fine-tuning HPs entirely.

- **(g) Protein + long-range generalization.** #6 answers with two *executed* reach tests (DeepLoc-2.0 protein, GLRB long-range enhancer); #1 adds GLRB long-range + DeepSTARR non-human regression; #2 instantiates the acc(s) law on a protein task + hashFrag's 196 kb curves.
